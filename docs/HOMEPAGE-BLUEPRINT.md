# BLX Solutions Homepage Blueprint

**Status: Initial blueprint agreed — 24 August 2026**

Official visual direction: [The Signal System, powered by BLX Compile transitions](DESIGN-DIRECTION.md).

## Homepage purpose

Within five seconds, a small local-business owner should understand:

1. BLX Solutions provides websites and digital marketing for businesses like theirs.
2. The work is supported by modern technology but directed by human strategy and judgment.
3. BLX offers a practical, low-pressure first step through a free website review.

## Primary message

**Leading idea:** Human-led digital strategy.

### Initial hero copy

**Eyebrow**

Websites and digital marketing for small local businesses

**Headline**

> Smarter digital tools.  
> Human-led strategy.  
> Better solutions.

**Supporting copy**

BLX Solutions combines web design, SEO, AI visibility and practical digital marketing with human judgment, clear strategy and an understanding of your business.

### Hero actions

1. **Primary:** Get a free website review
2. **Secondary:** Request a quote
3. **Supporting contact route:** Send a message

The three actions are all important, but they should not compete equally in the first viewport. The free review is the lowest-friction first step, the quote route supports visitors ready to buy, and messaging remains available in the navigation, contact section and footer.

## Proposed homepage structure

### 1. Navigation

- Current BLX logo
- Services
- Work
- About
- Contact
- Prominent **Request a quote** action
- Responsive mobile navigation

### 2. Kinetic hero

- Human-led strategy as the central message
- Selective kinetic typography
- Primary free-review action
- Secondary quote action
- Subtle animated blue light or connection-line motif
- Clean Signal System presentation

### 3. Trust and positioning strip

Three concise principles:

- Human-reviewed strategy
- Built for small local businesses
- Practical solutions, clearly explained

### 4. Services bento grid

Four primary service cards:

#### Website design

Fast, polished websites designed around the client's real business goals.

#### SEO and AI visibility

Help businesses become easier to discover through traditional search and emerging AI-assisted search.

#### Digital marketing

Practical campaigns, content direction and digital improvements chosen around the business rather than a generic checklist.

#### Website support and handover

Flexible ongoing help or a clear handover that teaches clients how to manage their own website confidently.

Each card should eventually use BLX Compile to transition into its genuine service-page URL.

### 5. Human-led approach

Explain BLX's central difference:

- Digital and AI tools improve efficiency.
- The tools do not decide the strategy.
- BLX applies human review, business understanding and brand judgment.
- Recommendations should solve a genuine problem rather than create unnecessary work.

### 6. Working process

A simple four-stage process:

1. Understand
2. Plan
3. Build
4. Improve

### 7. Selected work

A concise portfolio area containing real projects and honest project context. Initial examples can include the Circus Ibiza redesign and the grocery comparison prototype when they are ready to present.

### 8. Final conversion area

- Repeat **Get a free website review**
- Offer **Request a quote**
- Provide **Send a message**
- Keep the decision simple and low pressure

### 9. Footer

- Core navigation
- Contact information
- Social profiles
- Privacy and cookie information
- BLX Solutions business details when finalised

## Interaction hierarchy

The completed pages remain calm and readable. Technical spectacle is reserved for:

1. The first-session BLX Compile entry
2. Service-card-to-page Compile transitions
3. Kinetic hero typography
4. Subtle bento-card responses
5. The BLX connection-line motif

## Phase 1 content boundary

Phase 1 will build:

- Navigation
- Hero
- Trust strip
- Initial service bento grid
- One service-card micro-interaction
- Responsive and reduced-motion behaviour

Later phases will add the completed service pages, full BLX Compile system, portfolio detail, final contact handling and launch content.
